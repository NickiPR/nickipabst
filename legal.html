Cookies, privacy and terms

The short version: this website does not track you. No analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Below is exactly what happens when you visit, and the rules for using the site.

Cookies Privacy Terms of use

Cookies and what is stored on your device

This website sets no cookies of its own.

Cloudflare, which protects and delivers the site, may set a technical security cookie to keep bots out. It is strictly necessary and is not used for tracking.

The site stores one small item in your browser's local storage, called nickipabst_lang, to remember whether you chose English or Danish. It stays on your device, is never sent to me or anyone else, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Because nothing here needs your consent, there is no cookie banner.

Content from other companies

  • Booking calendar. When you reach the contact section, the booking calendar loads from Google Calendar. Google receives your IP address and browser details, and may set its own cookies. I do not control that. Read Google's privacy policy. If you would rather not load it, use the link under the calendar to open the booking page in a new tab, or contact me on LinkedIn.
  • Fonts, images and video. All of these are hosted on this website. Nothing is fetched from font or advertising networks.
  • Links. Links to LinkedIn and better.email take you to other websites with their own policies.

Privacy: what I collect and why

Who is responsible

Nicki Pabst, CVR 39554135, nickipabst.dk, is the data controller for the personal data described here. I do not publish an email address on purpose, because of spam. You can reach me on LinkedIn or through the booking calendar on the site.

What happens when you visit

I host the site myself on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and deliver it through Cloudflare, which handles DNS, caching and security. When a page is loaded, both receive your IP address and the details of the request, as every web service does. These technical logs are used to deliver and secure the site. I do not use them to identify you, and I do not run analytics on this website.

GitHub and Cloudflare are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

If you book a meeting

If you book through the calendar, Google collects the name, email address and any message you enter, and I receive it in order to hold the meeting. The basis is that you ask me to (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). I keep the details for as long as needed for the meeting and any follow-up, and then delete them.

If you contact me on LinkedIn

Your message is handled by LinkedIn under its own terms. I reply from there and use what you write only to answer you.

What I do not do

I do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it for marketing. There is no contact form and no newsletter on this website.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable format. Contact me and I will help. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency, Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby.

Privatliv: hvad jeg indsamler, og hvorfor

Hvem er ansvarlig

Nicki Pabst, CVR 39554135, nickipabst.dk, er dataansvarlig for de personoplysninger, der er beskrevet her. Jeg offentliggør med vilje ikke en e-mailadresse på grund af spam. Du kan kontakte mig på LinkedIn eller via bookingkalenderen på siden.

Hvad sker der, når du besøger siden

Jeg hoster siden selv på GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) og leverer den gennem Cloudflare, som tager sig af DNS, caching og sikkerhed. Booker du via kalenderen, indsamler Google dit navn, din e-mailadresse og en eventuel besked, og jeg modtager dem for at kunne holde mødet. Grundlaget er, at du selv beder om det (GDPR artikel 6, stk. 1, litra b). Jeg gemmer oplysningerne, så længe det er nødvendigt for mødet og en eventuel opfølgning, og sletter dem derefter.

Hvis du skriver til mig på LinkedIn

Din besked håndteres af LinkedIn efter deres egne vilkår. Jeg svarer derfra og bruger kun det, du skriver, til at svare dig.

Hvad jeg ikke gør

Jeg sælger ikke dine oplysninger, bruger dem ikke til annoncering og deler dem ikke til markedsføring. Der er hverken kontaktformular eller nyhedsbrev på denne hjemmeside.
